## Intent

Before implementing, settle one coherent, general architecture for the capabilities a real
brand job showed missing: curve-preserving geometry, stroke outlines and knockouts, variant
families with optical sizes, platform export targets, versioned kits, and review and
legibility tooling. Slice S00 of change 013; it unblocks S04 to S09. Design only: no code. The
owner asked for general, design-reviewed features rather than ad hoc logo plumbing, with every
decision carrying a recommendation and its trade-offs.

## Approach

The approved design lives in D-21 to D-27. Choices the owner and lead made:

- Persisted reuse in lockups: **baked placements with drift detection** (owner; live instances
  stay deferred). Rejected: live format-2 instances (large review and cost), copy by hand
  (silent drift).
- Kits from source builds: **allowed when stamped truthfully** (owner); `--require-bundle-producer`
  is a pinned, opt-in policy. Rejected: bundle-only kits (blocks brand changes between releases).
- Preview posture: **a static gallery regenerated by a command, no listener** (lead); a loopback
  server needs its own slice, security review and owner decision.
- Glyph-outline platform class: **open with the owner** (inbox); the recommendation, a declared
  per-platform class for `text outline` results, is the working assumption and gates only the
  text-outline increment of change 011.
- The design closure replaced a certified deviation bound with a tolerance measured on the emitted
  bytes and reported (D-21), kept sliver prevention as acceptance, removed the `joined` offset end
  type and unified the miter limit (declared cutovers).
